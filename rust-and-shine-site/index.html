Rust & Shine

Restoration · iPhone

Scrub old tools back to new.

Every workshop has a drawer of tools nobody has touched in years. Pull one out and bring it back: clear the grease, wire-brush the rust, then polish it until the light runs across it.

In development, coming to the App Store

What's inside

Eight objects

A wrench, claw hammer, pliers, brass padlock, kitchen knife, chrome hubcap, garden trowel and brass bell.

Three tools

Degrease, wire brush, polish. Each stage reveals the next, and each object fights back a little differently.

Sound that follows you

Scrub faster and the brush gets louder. Every sound is made live by the game, not a recording.

Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

What we collect

Clarke Studios does not collect your data. Rust & Shine has no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your progress, best times, sound and zen settings, and whether you bought Remove Ads, are saved on your device and are not sent to us. The game plays fully offline.

Ads

The iOS app can show optional rewarded video ads through Google AdMob. When you watch an ad (or, later, if a short full-screen ad is shown), the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect information such as a device identifier, advertising data, coarse location (including from IP address), crash and performance diagnostics, and interaction data such as video views. Google may use this for advertising and analytics. See How Google uses data from sites or apps that use our services.

Purchases (the one-time Remove Ads item) are handled by Apple. We do not see your payment details.

Tracking and consent

We do not track you ourselves. After you restore your first object, iOS may ask for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). You can play and watch rewarded videos without allowing tracking; if you decline, we request non-personalized ads. In the EEA, UK and other regions that require it, Google's consent form is shown before ads are initialized, and a Privacy options control stays on the workshop wall.

Remove Ads

A one-time in-app purchase removes ads and grants Today's spare and early barn-wall access without watching a video. If you have already purchased it, that flag is stored on your device so ads stay off even when you're offline. Use Restore purchases if you reinstall the app.

Children

Rust & Shine is a workshop restoration game, not a children's app, and it is not in the App Store Kids Category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. We do not treat the app as child-directed for ads.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details first.

How do I finish a stage?

Keep scrubbing until the meter reaches the end. The tool changes on its own when a stage is done.

How do I unlock the next object?

Finish the object before it. Finished pieces hang on the workshop wall, where you can replay them to beat your time.

Can I turn the sound off?

Yes. Tap the sound button on the workshop wall.

How do Today's spare and the barn wall work?

On the workshop wall, Today's spare offers a rewarded video that unlocks a bonus object for the rest of that day. If the barn tab is still locked, Work on the barn now offers a rewarded video to open it early. The main campaign stays fully playable by scrubbing, with or without ads.

How do I remove ads?

Buy Remove Ads, a one-time purchase. Restore purchases will recover it if you reinstall the app.
