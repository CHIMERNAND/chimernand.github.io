How do I play?

Tap a belt to turn it. When the arrows lead each part to the bin that wants it, press RUN. Some bins want parts that have been through the press (DONE), and some want them untouched (RAW). Press HINT if you are stuck.

My line jammed.

Two parts heading for the same spot both wait, and a part that runs off a belt or reaches the wrong bin jams the line. Re-aim the belts and press RUN again. You can also press STOP during a run, turn a belt, and carry on.

How do I unlock the next line?

Clear the one before it. Cleared lines stay open on the title screen, so you can replay them any time.

When do ads show?

Workshop Flow is free. A short ad can play when you leave the win card, at most once every three cleared levels. Ads never play during a level, and never after levels 1 or 2.

How do I remove ads or restore my purchase?

Tap the gear on the title screen. Remove Ads is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, and ads stop as soon as you buy it. On a new device or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchases.

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

Where consent is required (for example in the EEA and UK), the in-game Settings has a Privacy Choices button that reopens Google's consent form. To change tracking, open iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

How do I start over?

Settings > Reset Progress clears your cleared lines and tips. It does not undo a purchase.
