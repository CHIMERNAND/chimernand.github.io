Privacy

Last updated November 25, 2026.

Workshop Flow is a puzzle game for iPhone made by Clarke Studios. This policy explains what data the app uses and why.

What the game itself keeps

Clarke Studios does not collect your data. There are no accounts and no analytics of our own. Your progress (which lines you have cleared), your sound and haptics settings, which tips you have seen, and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved only on your device. We have no server of our own, so this information never reaches us. The game plays fully offline.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Workshop Flow is free and can show a full-screen ad between levels using Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC. To select, show and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK in the app may collect and send to Google:

  • device identifiers: the advertising identifier (IDFA), only if you allow tracking, and the identifier for vendors
  • advertising data, such as which ads were shown and tapped
  • product interaction data, such as app launches and taps
  • approximate (coarse) location, including from your IP address
  • crash, performance and other diagnostic data

Google may link some of this data to your device and use it for advertising and analytics. If you allow tracking, the advertising identifier may be used to show you personalized ads and to measure them across other companies' apps and websites. Google's consent tool (the User Messaging Platform) also uses approximate location, performance and interaction data to work out which consent rules apply and to show its form. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Consent and tracking

We do not track you ourselves. In the EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regions where it is required, Google's consent form is shown before any ad loads, and you can reopen it any time from Settings > Privacy Choices in the game. After that, iOS may ask whether Workshop Flow may track you (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not shared and the app requests only non-personalized ads. You can change this later in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

Remove Ads and purchases

Remove Ads is a one-time in-app purchase. Once you own it, the game stops loading and showing ads. Purchases are handled by Apple; we never see your payment details. The app asks Apple whether you own Remove Ads so it works after a reinstall or on another device, and keeps a note of it on your device so ads stay off when you're offline. Restore Purchases in Settings runs that check on demand.

Children

Workshop Flow is a puzzle game, not a children's app, and it is not in the App Store Kids category. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and we do not treat the app as child-directed for ads.

Changes

If a future version changes what is collected, we'll update this page and the App Store privacy details first, and change the date at the top.
