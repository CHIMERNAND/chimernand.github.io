Workshop Flow

Logic puzzle · iPhone

Fix the line. Watch it run.

The old workshop has gone quiet. Every line is broken: belts point the wrong way, presses sit idle and parts pile up. Tap a belt to turn it, press RUN and watch the parts ride the line. When every order is filled, the line is repaired, and another corner of the shop comes back to life.

What's inside

Twelve lines

Hand-made puzzles, from a single belt to a full shift with three colors, crates in the way and parts to sort.

One idea at a time

Turns, presses, raw and finished parts, merging lines that have to take turns, and stopping a running line to re-aim a belt.

Restore the workshop

The bench lamp, the belt, the lights, the press and finally the sign come back as you clear lines.
