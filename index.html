How we make games

i.

Your attention is not the product

No ads, no energy timers, no daily nags. You play when you want to, then put it down.

ii.

Your data stays yours

No analytics or tracking. When a game needs a server, it keeps only what the game needs, and you can delete it.

iii.

Prices you can see

One upfront price, or one clear unlock. No second currency and no loot boxes.

iv.

Tested by real players

Every game goes through rounds of TestFlight with friends and family before it ships.
